FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it upload my replays?

No. Replays are read and analysed on your own PC, and the AI coach is a model that runs locally too. Online, we only keep your email address, a hashed password and whether you're subscribed. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe.

Two small things do use the internet: the one-off download of the AI model, and the app checking for updates.

Could this get me banned?

RL Analyser only reads the replay files Rocket League saves to your computer, the same way replay-analysis websites do. It never touches the game itself: no memory reading, no overlays, no injection.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Rocket League on PC, from Epic or Steam. Replays saved on consoles can't be read. Mac and Linux aren't supported yet.

How do I get my replays in?

Switch on Rocket League's Auto-save replays setting. The app finds the replay folder for you, loads what's there, and picks up each new game as soon as it's saved.

Does the AI coach need a powerful PC?

It needs a one-off model download: about 2.7 GB for the Lite model, or 5.7 GB for the better Standard model (best with an 8 GB+ graphics card). With a graphics card a report takes seconds; on a CPU alone it takes a couple of minutes. Everything except the AI is instant either way.

How do I cancel?

Open the account menu in the app and choose Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the month you've paid for, then you're back on Free. Your replays and stats are never deleted.

Can I get a refund?

See the cancellation and refunds page for the details.

Why does Windows say “Unknown publisher”?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a caution the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway. We publish a SHA-256 checksum for every release so you can check your download is untouched.
