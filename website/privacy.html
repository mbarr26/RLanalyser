The short version. Your replays, your stats and the AI coach all stay on your own PC. Online we keep only your email address, a hashed password and whether you're subscribed. We don't run ads, sell data, or use analytics or tracking cookies on this website.

Who we are

RL Analyser is provided by [Your name or company] (“we”). You can reach us at support.

What stays on your PC

  • Your Rocket League replay files and everything the app works out from them (stats, key moments, goals, share images and clips).
  • Your settings, your chosen player and rank, and your chats with the AI coach.
  • The AI model you download and the engine that runs it. The coach does not send your matches or questions anywhere.
  • Your sign-in, kept in the Windows Credential Manager.

What we store online

  • Your email address, so you can sign in, confirm your account and reset your password.
  • A hashed password. We can't read it. Our account provider stores only a one-way hash.
  • Your subscription status: whether you're subscribed, and the date the paid period ends.
  • Technical logs kept briefly by our providers for security and abuse prevention (for example IP address and time of sign-in).

Payments

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe's page and never reach us or the app. We receive a customer reference and the subscription's status, and Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

Who else handles data (our processors)

  • Supabase: account sign-in and the subscription status table. [Region: add where your project is hosted.]
  • Stripe: payments, receipts and invoices.
  • [Email provider]: sending confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • [Website and download host]: serving this website and the installer.
  • Hugging Face: when you choose to download the AI model, the file comes from their servers, so they see your IP address like any download.

What we don't do

  • We don't upload or look at your replays.
  • We don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details until you delete your account. Deleting it (account menu › Delete my account) cancels your subscription and removes your email, password hash and subscription record. Stripe keeps invoices for as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, and you can complain to your data-protection authority. Email support and we'll respond within a reasonable time. We rely on contract (to provide the service you signed up for), legal obligation (tax and payment records) and legitimate interest (security).

Children

RL Analyser isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters we'll say so in the app or by email, and update the date above.
