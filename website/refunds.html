Cancellation & refunds

Last updated: [date]

Draft: this page was written from what the product actually does. Replace the [bracketed] parts, then have a lawyer look it over before you launch.

Cancel any time

Open RL Analyser, click your name at the top right, choose Manage subscription, and cancel on the Stripe page that opens. You keep Pro until the end of the month you've already paid for, then you're back on the Free plan. Nothing you saved is deleted.

Refunds

  • We don't give partial refunds for unused time in a month you chose not to cancel before it renewed.
  • If something went wrong, such as a charge you didn't mean to make, a duplicate payment, or a problem that stopped you using Pro, email support within [14] days and we'll sort it out, usually with a refund.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method and can take a few days to appear.

Your statutory rights

[Review with a lawyer before launch.] If you live in the EU or UK you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a digital purchase. When you start using Pro straight away you ask us to begin the service immediately and accept that this can end the withdrawal right once the service has started. Where the law gives you rights that can't be waived, they apply regardless of this page.

Problems with a payment

If a renewal fails, Stripe will retry for a short time and you'll see a banner in the app asking you to update your card. You can do that from Manage subscription.
